Corporate car rental is a specialized ground transportation service designed for businesses that need reliable, scalable mobility solutions. Unlike personal car rental, our platform offers centralized booking, unified invoicing, dedicated account management, real-time trip tracking, and compliance controls. We handle everything from daily employee commutes to C-suite executive travel, airport transfers, and large-scale event logistics — all managed through a single enterprise dashboard with custom billing and reporting.

We serve a wide range of industries including multinational corporations, law and consulting firms, financial services, healthcare and pharmaceutical companies, media and entertainment, technology companies, and more. Our platform is scalable enough for Fortune 500 enterprises with global operations and flexible enough for fast-growing startups needing reliable executive travel. Any organization that values punctuality, safety, and professional ground transportation can benefit from our services.

Executive Motion operates in 100+ cities across 6 continents, with key hubs in London, New York, Singapore, Mumbai, Dubai, and Sydney. Our global network ensures consistent service quality whether your team is traveling domestically or internationally. We continuously expand to new markets based on client demand and can arrange services in additional cities through our vetted partner network upon request.

Bookings can be made through our web platform, mobile app, or via your dedicated account manager. Employees can self-book within budget limits set by admins, or travel coordinators can manage all bookings centrally. Billing is fully unified — one monthly invoice per department, cost center, or the entire organization. We integrate directly with SAP Concur, Expensify, and Workday for automated expense reconciliation. Custom payment terms and corporate credit facilities are available for enterprise accounts.

There is no minimum trip volume or fleet size required. Our platform is designed to scale with your needs — from a single executive needing weekly airport transfers to a Fortune 500 company managing thousands of monthly trips across multiple countries. We offer flexible pricing models including pay-per-trip, monthly retainers, and enterprise annual contracts. Each plan includes dedicated account support and access to our full fleet and chauffeur network.

Safety is our top priority. Every chauffeur undergoes rigorous background checks, defensive driving certification, and ongoing performance reviews. Our fleet is maintained to the highest manufacturer standards with real-time vehicle diagnostics. The platform provides live trip tracking, SOS buttons, and automated check-ins for passenger safety. We hold ISO 9001 certification for quality management and ISO 27001 for information security, ensuring your corporate data and employee safety are always protected.

Our open API also allows custom integrations with your proprietary HR, travel management, or procurement platforms. Single Sign-On (SSO) via SAML 2.0 and OAuth is supported for seamless employee authentication. Our technical team provides full onboarding support to ensure smooth integration with your existing workflows.

Our fleet includes premium sedans (Mercedes S-Class, BMW 7 Series, Audi A8), luxury SUVs (Range Rover, Mercedes GLS), executive vans for group travel, and specialty vehicles for VIP and diplomatic transport. All vehicles are less than 2 years old, impeccably maintained, and equipped with Wi-Fi, charging ports, and refreshments. We also accommodate specific vehicle requests and can source specialty or armored vehicles for high-security requirements with advance notice.
