Corporate Billing FAQs

What is included in the 8hr / 80km package?

It includes vehicle usage, driver allowance, and fuel up to 80 kilometers or 8 hours. Tolls and state taxes are extra at actuals.

Are driver night charges applicable?

Night allowance (₹250 - ₹350) is applicable only if duty extends beyond 10:00 PM or starts before 6:00 AM.

Do you provide GST tax invoices?

Yes, all trips generate valid GST invoices with input tax credit eligibility for corporate accounts.
